## C. 発表で使う定型フレーズ（そのまま口に出せる形）

### C-1. 出だし・自己紹介
- "Thank you, Chair. My name is Shin Suzuki, from the Faculty of Economics at Fukuyama University, Japan."
- "I'm very glad to be in this stream, because the paper is exactly about **regional inequality as a fiscal question**."
- "I have about fifteen minutes, so let me go straight to the argument."（時間に触れて締まりを出す）

### C-2. 問いを立てる
- "The question of this paper is: **how did EU transfers, national grants and equalisation, and municipal finance interact** in Finland's peripheral regions after accession?"
- "The puzzle is not *whether* peripheral municipalities received EU money. They did. The puzzle is **what happens to the fiscal foundations of territorial service delivery** when an external, project-based transfer arrives precisely as the domestic, formula-based transfer system is being cut and redesigned."
- "Both processes acted on the same municipalities, **in opposite directions**, and were administered by **different ministries under different policy logics**."

### C-3. 先行研究の位置づけ（角を立てずにギャップを言う）
- "The additionality literature asks whether EU funds add to or replace national expenditure. It has taught us a great deal, but it works with **aggregate public expenditure** and tends to **leave the domestic institutional baseline unexamined**."
- "The welfare state rescaling literature has established that social policy is territorially mediated, but it has engaged **only lightly** with the grant and equalisation systems that make territorial provision fiscally possible."
- "My paper sits at the intersection of the two."

### C-4. 概念を導入する
- "I use the term **pseudo-complementarity** to describe a configuration in which EU transfers **formally satisfy** the principles of complementarity and additionality, **while the fiscal and governance context in which they land changes in ways those principles do not capture**."
- "The term has been used loosely in earlier work. What I try to do here is **separate it into three dimensions**, each with its own mechanism, its own evidence base, and its own limits."
- "And I want to be explicit about falsification: **for each dimension I specify what would have to be observed for it to hold, and what would count against it.**"
- "Pseudo-complementarity **in the strong sense requires all three**. In this case, **two hold and one does not**."

### C-5. データと方法
- "The evidence is of two kinds: **process tracing** of the documentary record, and a **municipality-level panel** I constructed from Statistics Finland's historical municipal finance statistics."
- "The panel covers **452 municipalities, 52 accounting functions, and the years 1997 to 1999** — about seventy thousand municipality-by-function-by-year cells."
- "Amounts are estimated with **Poisson pseudo-maximum-likelihood, with population as an exposure offset**, so that coefficients read as rate ratios per resident. PPML is used because the outcome contains **a large share of exact zeros**."
- "Let me state the limits **before** the results rather than after them." ← **効果的な先手**
- "**None of these estimates identifies a causal effect.** Objective 6 status is not randomly assigned, the panel is short, and it spans an accounting regime change."

### C-6. 歴史パートを速く語る
- "Two shocks arrived at once: **the collapse of the Soviet Union**, which made Western integration a security priority, and **a deep economic crisis**, with unemployment above eighteen per cent by 1994."
- "The decisive obstacle to accession was **agriculture**."
- "Objective 1 required GDP per capita **below seventy-five per cent** of the EU average — a threshold Finland's north and east did not meet."
- "So Protocol No. 6 did something analytically interesting: it **extended eligibility from measured economic backwardness to permanent sparsity** — eight inhabitants per square kilometre or fewer."
- "And the geography of that criterion **closely matched Finland's traditional support areas**."
- "In the referendum, **farmers voted against by more than ninety per cent**, and the north voted well below the national average. **The regions most opposed to accession were, in large part, the regions Objective 6 was designed to compensate.**"
- "I therefore read Objective 6 as a **politically constructed entitlement**."

### C-7. 国内層の話（ここが本稿の差別化点）
- "Now the part the additionality literature tends not to look at: **what was happening to the domestic layer at the same moment.**"
- "Until 1992, state grants were **task-specific and cost-based** — the state reimbursed a legally specified share of actual costs."
- "The 1993 reform replaced that with **prospective, criteria-based general grants**."
- "Two features matter. First, the reform **decoupled state funding from actual service costs** — grants became **fungible** revenue, and the state could adjust their volume **without legislating any change in service obligations**. Second, the reform was enacted **in the deepest trough of the fiscal crisis**, and one of its attractions for central government was precisely that it made grant volumes **controllable**."
- "**That controllability was used immediately.**" ← 短い決め台詞
- "The burden of adjustment fell **disproportionately** on municipalities with weak tax bases — the small, remote municipalities of the north and east, **which were simultaneously the designated beneficiaries of Objective 6**."
- "The reformed grant system was **not designed to accommodate substitution**, but it had the effect of making substitution **easier to effect and harder to observe**." ← **本稿で最も引用されやすい一文**

### C-8. 行政の分断
- "Additionality requires **an actor able to observe total public expenditure across sectors**. The Finnish implementation structure had a different character: fund management was **distributed across the Ministry of the Interior, the Ministry of Labour, and the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry**."
- "**Nobody was positioned to verify complementarity across sectors.** Each ministry executed EU funds as an extension of its own budget lines."
- "I want to be careful here: **I do not claim this structure was designed to obstruct verification.** What the record shows is a long-standing sectoral administration meeting a new cross-sectoral requirement. But its effect was that **the counterfactual against which additionality would have to be judged was not observed by anyone.**"

### C-9. 結果を提示する（不確実性つきで）
- "Finland received **1,652 million ECU** for 1995–99. The largest single share — **450 million, or 27 per cent** — went to Objective 6."
- "Nearly half of Objective 6, **46 per cent**, went to agriculture, forestry and rural development, under the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry. In other words, **the funds operated largely through the existing policy structure**."
- "Between 1993 and 1996 the relative decline was **concentrated in the general, non-earmarked component** — minus fifteen hundred markka per resident — with partial offsets in the sectoral components."
- "**Here I have to be careful.** The regression-adjusted estimates, controlling for tax base, population and region, **do not preserve the sign** of the unadjusted difference. So I report **the composition** of the contraction as robust, and I **do not** claim a conditionally larger reduction in Objective 6 municipalities. For the fiscal substitution dimension, this leaves the evidence **unresolved rather than confirmed**."
- "Over three years, EU-source subsidies amount to **406.9 million markka**, reaching **315 of 452 municipalities**. That is roughly **six to ten per cent** of the state's narrow sectoral subsidies, and **a fraction of one per cent** of total state grants."
- "**The scale disciplines every claim that follows: whatever else the EU layer did, it did not finance the welfare state.** Its interest lies in **its form and its targeting**, not its volume." ← **キーメッセージ**
- "Amounts were larger in Objective 6 municipalities **in point-estimate terms** — on the order of 87 per cent higher — but the confidence interval **runs from minus two to plus two hundred and fifty-eight per cent**, and therefore **includes no difference**. So I say the amounts are **larger in point estimates**; I do **not** say they are **concentrated** there."
- "For welfare state analysis, **this is the most consequential result**: core welfare, health and social services received **9.2 per cent** of the total."
- "The mirror image is economic and business development, where the Objective 6 association is **the largest and the only one that survives multiple-testing adjustment** — a rate ratio of about seven. Even here the interval is very wide, so the estimate establishes **the sign of the association, not its magnitude**."
- "I describe this as **a functional division of labour between the layers**: **the EU layer did not finance the welfare services of the periphery. It financed development.**"

### C-10. ゼロ結果を語る
- "Did EU money visibly replace state subsidies within the same municipality and function? **At ledger level, the answer is no.**"
- "**Genuine addition** — EU up, state subsidies not down — accounts for **64.7 per cent** of cells. **Strict apparent complementarity** — an EU increase approximately offsetting a state decrease — accounts for **1.3 per cent**."
- "Nor is the correlation negative, as crowding out would imply: it is **approximately plus 0.05**."
- "**The negative result is as informative as the positive ones, but it must be read carefully.** If pseudo-complementarity had operated as a municipal-ledger swap, this type would be common. It is rare. **What follows is that this mechanism is not supported. It does not follow that substitution occurred elsewhere.**"
- "**The absence of ledger-level offsetting is equally consistent with substitution in the state budget — which municipal accounts cannot observe — and with no substitution having taken place.**"

### C-11. 締め
- "Let me close with what the concept earns and what it does not."
- "**Two of the three dimensions are supported here. The third — fiscal substitution — is not established by this evidence.** The contribution of the concept is therefore **analytical rather than evidentiary**: it separates dimensions that are routinely conflated, and it specifies what each would require to be shown."
- "The generalisable proposition, stated cautiously, is this: in service-based welfare states with decentralised delivery, external transfers can **leave the aggregate fiscal effect indeterminate at the level where the transfer is recorded, while changing the governance form of territorial finance in ways that are directly observable**."
- "And that has a live implication: **assessments of cohesion policy — and, prospectively, of instruments like the Recovery and Resilience Facility — that ignore the domestic intergovernmental baseline risk misreading both their distributive and their institutional effects.**"
- "Thank you. I look forward to your comments."

## F. 言い換えの引き出し（同じことを別の言い方で）

本番で単語が出てこないとき用。

| 言いたいこと | 引き出し |
|---|---|
| 国内の補助金が減っていた | *the domestic layer was contracting* / *state grants were being cut* / *the baseline was shrinking* |
| 使途が自由になった | *grants became fungible* / *the money was no longer tied to actual costs* / *the strings came off* |
| 誰も監視していなかった | *nobody was positioned to verify it* / *no institution was charged with constructing the counterfactual* / *the counterfactual was not observed by anyone* |
| 推定が不精確 | *imprecisely estimated* / *the interval is wide* / *the interval includes no difference* / *I report the sign, not the magnitude* |
| 因果ではない | *this is descriptive, not causal* / *I don't identify a causal effect* / *no causal claim is made here* |
| EU資金は福祉に行かなかった | *the EU layer did not finance welfare services* / *core welfare received under a tenth of it* / *the money went to development, not to services* |
| 形式が変わった | *the governance form changed* / *from formula to competition* / *from entitlement to application* |
| 上流で起きたかもしれない | *if leakage occurred, it must have occurred further upstream* / *at a higher level, in the state budget* |
