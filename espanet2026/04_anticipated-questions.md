## A. 最も来る可能性が高い10問（この10問は暗記に近い状態にしておく）

### A1. 結局、代替はあったのか？

**Q**: "So — did substitution happen or not? Your title suggests it, but your results seem to say no."

**狙い**：本稿の最大の緊張点。ここで曖昧に答えると論文全体の信頼が落ちる。**きっぱりと「立証していない」と言い、しかし何が言えるかを示す**のが正解。

**A（英語）**:
> That's the central question, and my answer is: **I do not demonstrate substitution, and I don't claim to.** What I can say is more specific and, I think, more useful. **One mechanism is excluded**: if pseudo-complementarity had operated as a municipal-ledger swap — EU money in, state subsidy out, line by line — that pattern would be common in my data. It accounts for **1.3 per cent of cells**, and for none inside the Objective 6 area. And the correlation between changes in EU and state subsidies is **slightly positive, not negative**. So the ledger mechanism is out. **But a null at the municipal level is uninformative about the state budget** — they are different scales. Upstream substitution remains a **hypothesis compatible with the observed sequence**, not a finding. That's why the paper reports it in a section explicitly labelled hypotheses for further testing.

**骨子**：代替は立証していないし、そう主張もしない。ただし**自治体台帳スワップという特定のメカニズムは排除された**（1.3%、目標6域内ゼロ、相関+0.05）。しかし自治体レベルのゼロは国家予算については無情報——スケールが違う。上流代替は**観察された系列と両立する仮説**であってfindingではない。だから「今後の検証のための仮説」節に置いている。

### A2. 調整推定が符号を反転するなら、知見は残るのか？

**Q**: "You say the regression-adjusted estimates don't preserve the sign of the unadjusted difference. Doesn't that just mean the finding disappears?"

**狙い**：8.1の最も弱い部分。**自分から先に開示している**ことを示すのが効く。

**A（英語）**:
> **It means one finding disappears and another survives, and I try to be precise about which is which.** What does *not* survive is the claim that Objective 6 municipalities experienced a **conditionally larger** reduction in state grants. I explicitly do not make that claim. What **does** survive is a claim about **composition**: the contraction was concentrated in the **general, non-earmarked component** — the least conditional part of the transfer system, and the part on which sparsely populated municipalities depended most. That is an accounting fact about the decomposition, and it is exactly additive within the matched sample. So for the fiscal substitution dimension, I report the domestic-baseline evidence as **unresolved rather than confirmed** — and I say so in the paper rather than letting a reader discover it.

**骨子**：消えるのは「目標6で条件付きにより大きな削減」という主張——それは主張していない。残るのは**構成**についての主張：収縮は**一般・非特定財源部分**（最も条件が緩く、希薄自治体が最も依存していた部分）に集中した。これは分解についての会計的事実で、突合標本内で厳密に加法的。だから財政的代替次元は**未決**と報告している。

### A3. 「EUから」という会計項目は構造基金ではないのでは？

**Q**: "Your EU variable is what municipalities *booked* as EU money. That's not the Structural Funds. How much does that undermine the analysis?"

**A（英語）**:
> You're right, and I say so explicitly as a measurement boundary. The item **may include other EU programmes**, and it **excludes Structural Fund money that never passed through a municipal budget** — which is a lot of it, because regional councils, enterprises and associations were major beneficiaries. So I don't call it a measure of Structural Fund disbursement. **I call it an indicator of the EU layer as it reached municipal ledgers** — which is precisely the object I need, because my question is what the units that actually deliver services recorded. The claim is about municipal accounts, and the measure matches the claim. It would be a serious problem if I were estimating programme-level additionality; I explicitly am not, and the paper says the estimates do not constitute a test of the EU's official additionality verification, which is defined over total eligible public expenditure at programme level.

**骨子**：その通りで、測定境界として明示している。他のEUプログラムを含みうるし、自治体予算を通らなかった構造基金は除外される。だから「構造基金支出の計測」とは呼ばず、**「自治体台帳に届いた限りでのEU層の指標」**と呼ぶ。問いがサービス供給主体の会計記録についてである以上、測定は主張に対応している。プログラム段階の追加性を推定しているなら深刻だが、そうではないと明記している。

### A4. 自治体連合（kuntayhtymät）を落としているのは致命的では？

**Q**: "Joint municipal authorities deliver specialised health care and vocational education. You've excluded exactly the welfare-heavy part of the system. Doesn't that bias your 9.2 per cent figure downwards?"

**狙い**：北欧の福祉国家研究者なら必ず気づく。**方向性のあるバイアスとして認め、しかし主張を守る**。

**A（英語）**:
> That's the sharpest version of the data objection, and yes — joint municipal authorities are **outside the data by construction**, and I flag it as one of three measurement boundaries. It could bias the welfare share downwards, and I would not defend 9.2 per cent as *the* welfare share of EU money in Finland. What I'd say is this. **First**, the claim I make is about **municipal accounts**, not about the Finnish welfare system as a whole. **Second**, the comparative statement — welfare low, economic development high, and the Objective 6 association concentrated in development — is **within the same data**, so the exclusion affects both sides of the comparison rather than only one. **Third**, if anything, joint authorities were funded through the same national sectoral channels that were being cut, so including them would, if I had to guess, sharpen the division-of-labour picture rather than dissolve it. But that is a guess, and the honest position is that it is untested. Getting kuntayhtymä accounts into the panel is on my list for the next stage.

**骨子**：最も鋭いデータ批判。その通りで構成上データ外であり、3つの測定境界の一つとして明示している。9.2%が「フィンランドにおけるEUマネーの福祉シェア」だとは擁護しない。ただし①主張は自治体会計についてであり福祉システム全体についてではない、②比較（福祉低・開発高）は同一データ内なので除外は比較の両側に効く、③自治体連合は削減されつつあった同じ国内部門チャネルで賄われていたので、含めれば分業の像はむしろ鮮明になる可能性が高い——ただしこれは推測で、未検証。次段階の課題。

### A5. 信頼区間が広すぎて何も言えないのでは？

**Q**: "A confidence interval from plus 70 to plus 2,744 per cent. What does that estimate actually tell us?"

**A（英語）**:
> It tells us **the sign, and nothing about the magnitude** — and that is exactly how I report it. I say the rate ratio is about seven, and then I say in the same sentence that the interval establishes the sign of the association, **not** its size. What makes the sign worth reporting is that **it is the only function-group association that survives false-discovery-rate adjustment** across the seven groups. So the defensible statement is: **within EU-source municipal receipts, the Objective 6 surplus is a development surplus, not a welfare surplus.** That is a statement about direction and composition, and the data support it. Anyone who wants a magnitude needs project-level data, which is the same conclusion I reach about the substitution question.

**骨子**：符号だけを教え、大きさについては何も教えない——そう報告している。符号を報告する価値があるのは、7群の中で**FDR調整を生き延びる唯一の関連**だから。擁護可能な言明は「EU由来の自治体受取の中で、目標6の余剰は**開発の余剰であって福祉の余剰ではない**」。大きさが欲しければプロジェクト段階のデータが要る。

### A6. 3年のパネルで何が言えるのか？

**Q**: "Three years, 1997 to 1999. That's the tail end of the programming period. Why should we believe this captures anything?"

**A（英語）**:
> Three constraints forced that window, and I'd rather name them than defend around them. **The accounting regime changed in 1997**, so 1993–96 and 1997–99 are not a continuous series — I analyse changes within each period and never compute growth rates across the boundary. **The programming period ended in 1999**, so 1997–99 is the second half of it — which, if anything, is when disbursement was heaviest, since Structural Fund payments lag commitments. And I make no trend claim: I make claims about **level, dispersion and functional composition** within the window, which three years can support. What three years cannot support is anything dynamic — which is why behavioural adaptation appears in the paper as a hypothesis requiring application and project-participation data over a longer horizon, not as a finding.

**骨子**：3つの制約が窓を決めた。①1997年に会計制度が変わったので93–96と97–99は連続系列ではなく、境界をまたぐ成長率は計算しない。②プログラム期間は1999年終了で、97–99はその後半——支払は約束に遅れるので、むしろ支出が最も厚い時期。③トレンドの主張はしていない。主張は窓内の**水準・分散・機能構成**についてで、3年で支持できる。3年で支持できないのは動学——だから行動的適応は仮説として置いている。

### A7. 目標6ステータスは内生的では？

**Q**: "Objective 6 status was assigned on population density, which correlates with everything. Isn't your comparison meaningless?"

**A（英語）**:
> The comparison is **descriptive, and I never treat it as identification.** Objective 6 status correlates with geography, industrial structure and demography, and I say so. What I'd add is that the assignment rule is unusually **transparent**: eight inhabitants per square kilometre or fewer, fixed in Protocol No. 6, time-invariant over my window. That doesn't make it exogenous — but it does mean the selection mechanism is **known and stated**, rather than a black box. And note what the paper does with it: it uses the contrast to describe **where money went**, not to estimate what the money **achieved**. For the latter you'd want a design exploiting the density threshold, and Vehkasalo's work on Finnish regional policy goes in that direction. That's a genuinely different paper from this one.

**骨子**：比較は**記述的**で、同定として扱っていない。地理・産業構造・人口と相関することは明記。ただし割当ルールは異例に**透明**（8人/km²、議定書6で固定、窓内で時間不変）。外生性は与えないが、選択メカニズムは既知で明示されている。そして本稿はこの対比を「マネーがどこへ行ったか」の記述に使い、「何を達成したか」の推定には使わない。後者には密度閾値を使った設計が要る（Vehkasaloの方向）。それは別の論文。

### A8. これは福祉国家研究なのか、財政研究なのか？

**Q**: "You measure no welfare outcome. In what sense is this a welfare state paper?"

**狙い**：ESPAnetという場での正統性への問い。**先回りして自分から言っているのが強み**。

**A（英語）**:
> It's a fair challenge, and my answer is that **the fiscal architecture is a condition of territorial welfare capacity, and conditions are worth studying in their own right.** In a service welfare state where roughly 450 municipalities carry primary responsibility for provision, whether social rights are effective across space depends on the grant and equalisation system that finances delivery. That system is under-examined in welfare state research, which has established that social policy is territorially mediated but engaged only lightly with intergovernmental fiscal relations. **What I do not do is infer outcomes from flows** — I say explicitly that I observe no access, quality, staffing or coverage, and that statements about "capacity" should be read as statements about its fiscal and institutional underpinning. Linking the fiscal panel to sectoral output and personnel data is the third of the three hypotheses I list for further work, and I'd be glad to hear suggestions on data sources for it.

**骨子**：財政アーキテクチャは領域的福祉能力の**条件**であり、条件はそれ自体研究に値する。約450自治体が供給責任を負うサービス型福祉国家では、社会権の空間的実効性は供給を支える補助金・財政調整制度に依存する。その制度は福祉国家研究で未検討。**フローから成果を推論することはしない**——アクセス・質・人員は観察しないと明記し、「capacity」はその財政的・制度的前提を指すと述べている。財政パネルと部門別産出・人員データのリンクが3番目の仮説。

### A9. pseudo-complementarity は新しい概念なのか？

**Q**: "Isn't 'pseudo-complementarity' just additionality failure with a new label?"

**A（英語）**:
> If it were only a label, I'd agree it wouldn't earn its place. The work it does is **disaggregation**. Additionality failure is a single verdict: funds did or did not add to national spending. My claim is that **three things are routinely conflated in that verdict**. A member state can **satisfy the formal requirements of additionality while the domestic baseline shifts underneath** — those are different objects. And it can **change the governance form of territorial finance without any substitution occurring at all** — which is, on my evidence, close to what Finland did. Separating the dimensions has a practical payoff: it tells you what each would require to be shown, and it stops you from reading a change of form as evidence of substitution. In fact the concept **constrains my own conclusion**: because fiscal substitution isn't established, I'm not entitled to call this pseudo-complementarity in the strong sense, and I don't.

**骨子**：ラベルだけなら不要。概念が果たすのは**分解**。追加性の失敗は単一の判定だが、そこには3つのものが混同されている。加盟国は**追加性の形式要件を満たしながら国内ベースラインを動かす**ことができ、また**代替なしに領域財政のガバナンス形式を変える**こともできる——本稿の証拠ではフィンランドは後者に近い。分離の実益は、各次元に何が必要かを示し、形式変化を代替の証拠と誤読させないこと。実際この概念は**自分の結論を制約している**：財政的代替が立証されない以上、強い意味での疑似補完性とは呼べないし、呼んでいない。

### A10. 政策的にどう使えるのか？

**Q**: "What should a policymaker or an evaluator take from this?"

**A（英語）**:
> One operational point above all: **an additionality assessment that does not include the domestic intergovernmental baseline can be satisfied while the aggregate fiscal position of assisted regions deteriorates.** In Finland the baseline was being cut for consolidation reasons at the same moment, and no institution was tasked with observing it across sectors — so the counterfactual against which additionality would have to be judged **was not observed by anyone.** That's not a Finnish peculiarity; it's a design gap. And it is **live**: instruments like the Recovery and Resilience Facility land in national systems that are themselves being reformed, and evaluations that ignore the domestic grant and equalisation layer risk misreading both the distributive and the institutional effects. The concrete recommendation is that verification should be defined **jointly over the external transfer and the domestic transfer system**, and that someone should hold the horizontal mandate to do it.

**骨子**：**国内の政府間ベースラインを含まない追加性評価は、被支援地域の集計的財政状況が悪化していても満たされうる。** フィンランドでは同時に緊縮でベースラインが削られ、部門横断でそれを観察する任務を負う機関がなかった——反事実を誰も観察していなかった。これはフィンランド固有ではなく設計上の欠落で、RRFなど現在の手段にも直結する。具体的提言は、検証を**外部移転と国内移転制度の両方について統合的に**定義し、それを行う水平的権限を誰かに持たせること。

## B. 方法・統計への質問

### B1. なぜPPMLか
**Q**: "Why PPML rather than a two-part model, a Tobit, or log(1+y)?"
**A**: > The outcome is **municipality × function cells, and a large share are exact zeros** — most municipalities receive nothing in most functions in most years. PPML handles that **without dropping observations and without transforming the dependent variable**, and with population as an exposure offset the coefficients read directly as **rate ratios per resident**, which is the quantity I want. A log(1+y) transformation makes the coefficient depend on the units of the constant, and a two-part model would answer a different question — I actually report the participation margin separately, as a difference in proportions with a confidence interval, so both margins are in the paper. A hurdle specification would be a reasonable robustness check and I'd be happy to add it.
**骨子**：ゼロが大量。PPMLは観測を落とさず被説明変数も変換しない。人口オフセットで係数が1人当たり率比になる。log(1+y)は定数の単位に依存する。二段階モデルは別の問いだが、参加マージンは比率差＋CIとして別途報告済み。ハードル・モデルは合理的な頑健性チェックで、追加は歓迎。

### B2. 固定効果は？
**Q**: "Do you include year and function fixed effects?"
**A**: > The specification detail is one of the things I'm finalising for the final version, and I'd rather tell you what's in the current estimates than improvise. The core specification is EU-source subsidies on the Objective 6 indicator with a population exposure offset and municipality-clustered standard errors; the function-group results in Table 6 are estimated **within** function group, which absorbs cross-function level differences by construction. **What I can't do with a time-invariant treatment is municipality fixed effects** — Objective 6 status doesn't vary within municipality over the window, so it would be absorbed. That's a real constraint of the design and one reason the estimates are descriptive.
**骨子**：仕様の詳細は最終版で確定中なので、現在の推定に入っているものだけを述べる。中核仕様は目標6ダミー＋人口オフセット＋自治体クラスター。表6は機能群**内**推定なので機能間の水準差は構成上吸収される。**時間不変の処理変数のため自治体固定効果は使えない**（吸収されてしまう）。これは設計上の実質的制約で、推定が記述的である理由の一つ。
> ⚠️ **注意**：論文には「[AUTHOR TO CONFIRM: 正確なPPML仕様——年固定効果と共変量、FDR手続、ソフトウェア]」が残っている。**発表前に確定させ、上の回答をその内容に合わせて書き換えること。** 確定していない場合は上記のように「最終版で確定中」と述べ、その場での断定を避ける。

### B3. FDRの手続と検定族
**Q**: "Which FDR procedure, and over what family of tests?"
**A**: > Across the seven harmonised function groups, and I report both unadjusted and adjusted p-values in the table so readers can see what adjustment does. The procedure and its exact family are among the specification details I'm pinning down for the final version. What matters for the substantive reading is the ordering: **economic and business development is the only association that survives adjustment**, and the welfare association weakens under it — which is why I label the latter suggestive rather than significant.
**骨子**：7つの調和機能群にわたって適用し、未調整・調整の両方を表に出している。手続と検定族の厳密な指定は最終版で確定中。実質的読解に効くのは順序：**経済・事業開発だけが調整を生き延び**、福祉の関連は弱まる——だから後者を「示唆的」と表示している。

### B4. ±20%という閾値は恣意的では？
**Q**: "The ±20 per cent band, the ±3 per cent variant, the p10 threshold — these look like researcher degrees of freedom."
**A**: > They are choices, and I try to make them **visible rather than invisible**. The ±20 per cent band exists because **municipal accounts are not designed to record substitution**, so offsetting, if it happened, would be approximate rather than exact. The p10 threshold removes trivial movements that would otherwise dominate a cell-level classification. And I report the ±3 per cent variant as a sensitivity check — **the difference between the two is substantial, and I say in the paper that this is itself informative about the fragility of the classification.** The result I lean on is robust to the choice in the direction that matters: strict apparent complementarity is rare under the *permissive* band, which is the band most favourable to finding it. Tightening it can only make it rarer.
**骨子**：選択であることを**見えるようにしている**。±20%は自治体会計が代替を記録するようには作られていないから（相殺は厳密でなく近似的なはず）。p10閾値は些末な変動を除く。±3%版は感度分析として報告し、**両者の差が大きいこと自体が分類の脆弱性についての情報だと論文に書いている**。依拠している結果は、重要な方向では選択に頑健：**最も緩い帯（＝見つかりやすい帯）でも strict apparent complementarity は稀**であり、締めれば稀になるだけ。

### B5. 標本が452→399→384と動くのはなぜか
**Q**: "Why do your samples change size across tables?"
**A**: > Because the 1993–96 component data cannot be matched for every municipality. The **full sample is 452**; the **decomposable mainland sample of 399** is those for which all four grant components can be matched and summed exactly for both 1993 and 1996; and the **endpoint-matched sample of 384** is used for the year-by-year trajectory, where a municipality must be present in every year. **I report the sensitivity openly** — the full-sample total is minus 161.8 markka and the decomposable-sample total is minus 21.5 — and I treat that gap as a reason to rest the argument on the **composition** of the change rather than its magnitude.
**骨子**：93–96年の構成要素データが全自治体で突合できないため。全標本452／両年で4要素すべてが厳密に突合・合計できる分解可能本土標本399／全年在籍を要する端点突合標本384（年次軌跡用）。**感度は公開している**（全標本 −161.8 vs 分解可能標本 −21.5）。この差があるからこそ、議論を大きさではなく**変化の構成**に置いている。

## C. 理論・概念への質問

### C1. Brennerを使う必然性は？
**Q**: "Could you make the same argument without Brenner? What does state rescaling add that fiscal federalism doesn't?"
**A**: > Fiscal federalism gives me the **mechanics** — what matching and lump-sum grants do, why fungibility matters, why the flypaper literature expects stickiness. What it doesn't give me is a way to describe **a change in the architecture itself**. Brenner's point is that rescaling is not decentralisation or centralisation but **the reorganisation of the scalar architecture through which state functions are financed and delivered**: new scales inserted, existing scales redefined, spatial targeting shifted. That is precisely what happened here — **a new scale (the EU layer), a redefined one (regional councils as receiving organisations), and a changed criterion of entitlement, from service cost and fiscal capacity to permanent geographical sparsity.** Fiscal federalism can't name that; it can only price the individual instruments.
**骨子**：財政連邦主義は**メカニクス**（定率/一括補助金の働き、代替可能性、フライペーパー）を与えるが、**アーキテクチャそのものの変化**を記述する語彙は与えない。ブレナーのリスケーリングは分権/集権ではなく**国家機能が財政・供給されるスケール的アーキテクチャの再編**——新しいスケールの挿入、既存スケールの再定義、空間的標的の移動。本事例はまさにそれ（EU層の挿入、受入組織としての地域議会、受給要件がサービス費用・財政力から恒久的希薄性へ）。

### C2. これは retrenchment ではないのか？
**Q**: "Grants were cut, services were under pressure. Why not just call it retrenchment?"
**A**: > Because retrenchment describes a change in **volume**, and what I observe is a change in **composition, conditionality and spatial logic** as well. The resources sustaining peripheral provision were not simply reduced. **A function historically performed by central transfers through distributive, legally standardised channels came to be partly performed by EU transfers through competitive, project-based, time-limited channels.** Calling that retrenchment would miss the part of the story that has the clearest evidence behind it — the administrative transformation. And it would also over-claim on the part that doesn't: I'd be asserting a net reduction in resources to these regions, which my adjusted estimates don't support.
**骨子**：retrenchmentは**量**の変化を記述するが、観察されるのは**構成・条件性・空間的論理**の変化でもある。中央移転が分配的・法的に標準化されたチャネルで担ってきた機能を、EU移転が競争的・プロジェクト型・期限付きのチャネルで部分的に担うようになった。retrenchmentと呼べば**最も証拠のある部分（行政的変容）を取り落とし**、同時に**証拠のない部分（純減）を過剰主張する**ことになる。

### C3. フライペーパー効果との関係
**Q**: "How does your result speak to the flypaper literature?"
**A**: > It suggests **where stickiness and leakage might be located.** At municipal level the money that arrived largely stayed: changes in EU-source and state subsidies were essentially uncorrelated, and in the Objective 6 area EU inflows were weakly associated with maintained functional expenditure. **That's stickiness at the point of receipt.** If leakage occurred, it must have occurred at a higher level — where a hard consolidation constraint, a fungible grant interface and the absence of a counterfactual monitor would make external transfers absorbable as replacement finance. I want to be clear that this is **consistent with the institutional record but not tested here.** What the case does show independently is that **the form of a transfer has effects independent of its volume.**
**骨子**：**粘着と漏出がどこに位置しうるか**を示唆する。自治体段階では到着した資金はおおむね留まった（無相関、機能的支出の維持と弱い関連）＝**受領点での粘着**。漏出があったなら上位水準（硬い緊縮制約・代替可能な界面・反事実監視の不在）で起きたはず。これは制度的記録と整合的だが**検証されていない**。独立に示せるのは、**移転の形式が量とは独立に効く**こと。

### C4. 多層ガバナンス論への含意
**Q**: "Hooghe and Marks would predict subnational empowerment. You find the opposite. How strong is that claim?"
**A**: > I'd put it as **compatibility rather than refutation**, because I have institutional evidence, not a governance measurement. The record shows regional councils were **not passive** — they lobbied over the next programming period and prepared their own proposals — but their position was weak: **strict verification by the Commission and the Court of Auditors, combined with delays and inconsistent judgements among several funding ministries, constrained regional autonomy.** Decision-making power remained largely with central ministries. So the Finnish configuration is **compatible with an arrangement in which central government retained control over the EU layer while the regional layer remained dependent** — partnership functioning as a channel for centrally defined priorities rather than as EU-style multilevel governance. In Kazepov's terms: **multilevel in form, centrally steered in substance.**
**骨子**：反証ではなく**両立性**として述べる（制度的証拠であってガバナンスの計測ではないため）。地域議会は受動的ではなかったが立場は弱く、委員会・会計検査院の厳格な検証と複数省庁間の遅延・不一致が自律性を制約した。決定権は中央省庁に残った。よってフィンランドの配置は「中央がEU層の統制を保持し地域層は依存的」という取り決めと両立する。カゼポフ的には**形式は多層、実質は中央操舵**。

## D. 事例・比較への質問

### D1. なぜフィンランドか（事例選択）
**Q**: "Why Finland? Is it a typical case or an outlier?"
**A**: > It's a **strategic case, not a typical one**, and I choose it for two reasons. **First, the coincidence**: crisis, grant reform and accession happened in the same few years, which is what makes the interaction observable at all — in most member states the domestic baseline is stable enough that you can't see it moving. **Second, the data**: Finnish municipal accounts allow all three layers — municipal finance, state grants, EU receipts — to be observed **at the level of the units that actually deliver services**, by function. That combination is rare. The cost of strategic case selection is generalisability, which is why the paper states **scope conditions** rather than claiming general validity: the argument applies most directly where welfare services are delivered by **fiscally significant local government**, and where an external transfer arrives **during** a domestic reform of intergovernmental transfers.
**骨子**：**典型例ではなく戦略的事例**。理由は①同時性（危機・補助金改革・加盟が数年内に起きたので相互作用が観察可能になる。多くの加盟国ではベースラインが安定していて動きが見えない）、②データ（3層すべてを、実際に供給する単位のレベルで、機能別に観察できる）。代償は一般化可能性なので、**適用範囲条件**を明示している：福祉サービスが**財政的に重要な地方政府**によって供給され、外部移転が国内の政府間移転改革の**最中に**到着する場合。

### D2. スウェーデンとの比較
**Q**: "Sweden received Objective 6 under the same protocol. Why not compare?"
**A**: > **That's exactly the comparison I want to make next, and I think it's the right test of the argument.** Sweden received a smaller Objective 6 allocation — 247 million ECU, 17.9 per cent of its total, against Finland's 450 million and 27.2 per cent — **under the same protocol but with a different domestic grant regime**. If the configuration I describe is driven by the EU layer, Sweden should look similar; if it's driven by the domestic reform and consolidation, Sweden should look different. That is a **clean discriminating test**, and it's the third item on my list of next steps. The obstacle is comparable municipal accounting data at function level, which is a real data problem rather than a design one.
**骨子**：**まさに次にやりたい比較で、議論の正しいテスト**。スウェーデンは同じ議定書の下で目標6配分が小さい（247百万ECU、17.9% vs フィンランド450百万、27.2%）が、**国内補助金レジームが異なる**。配置がEU層に駆動されているならスウェーデンも似るはず、国内改革と緊縮に駆動されているなら異なるはず——**きれいな識別テスト**。障害は機能レベルで比較可能な自治体会計データ（設計ではなくデータの問題）。

### D3. 「places that don't matter」との接続
**Q**: "You cite the 'places that don't matter' framing. Is there a link to the rise of the Finns Party?"
**A**: > There is a link **in the literature I draw on**, and I'm careful not to overstate what my own data can say. Mattila, Purkarthofer and Humer report that peripheral regions such as Kainuu and Lapland were perceived by central elites as places that don't matter, and they connect that to later populist mobilisation. **My contribution to that story is fiscal rather than electoral**: what the accounts show is that the periphery received development money through competitive channels while its service finance came from a national layer that was being cut — and that regional councils operated as receiving organisations rather than as partners in policy formation. **Whether that fed political resentment is a question my data cannot answer** — but I'd note it connects directly to Zhen Jie Im's paper in this session on regional knowledge economies and populist right voting, and I'd be interested in his view.
**骨子**：**引用している文献の中に**リンクがあり、自分のデータが言えること以上には述べない。Mattilaらは周辺地域が中央エリートから「重要でない場所」と見られていたと報告し、後のポピュリスト動員に接続している。**本稿の寄与は選挙的ではなく財政的**：周辺部は競争的チャネルで開発資金を受け取る一方、サービス財源は削減されつつある国内層から来ており、地域議会は政策形成のパートナーではなく受入組織として作動した。**それが政治的怨嗟を育てたかは本稿のデータでは答えられない**——ただし本セッションのIm報告と直結するので意見を聞きたい。（※セッション内の他報告に橋を架けると議論が伸びる）

### D4. 日本への含意
**Q**: "You're at a Japanese university. Does this travel to Japan?"
**A**: > It's a question I think about, and the scope conditions give the answer. Japan **satisfies the first condition strongly**: welfare services are delivered by fiscally significant local government, and there is a well-developed equalisation system in the local allocation tax. What Japan lacks is **the second condition — an external transfer layer**; there is no EU. So the direct analogue doesn't exist. **The transferable part is the analytical one**: when central government reforms intergovernmental transfers from cost-based to general-purpose while simultaneously reducing their volume, **the counterfactual for local spending becomes unobservable**, and any earmarked programme layered on top can be presented as supplementary regardless of what happens to the baseline. That configuration is not specific to the EU, and Japanese local finance has had episodes with the same shape. I'd be glad to talk about it afterwards.
**骨子**：適用範囲条件が答えを与える。日本は**第一条件を強く満たす**（財政的に重要な地方政府＋地方交付税という発達した財政調整）。欠けるのは**第二条件＝外部移転層**（EUがない）。したがって直接の対応物はない。**移転可能なのは分析的部分**：中央政府が政府間移転を費用ベースから一般財源へ改革しつつ同時に総量を削減すると、**地方支出の反事実が観察不可能になり**、その上に載る特定財源プログラムは、ベースラインに何が起きようと「補完的」と提示できてしまう。この配置はEU固有ではなく、日本の地方財政にも同型のエピソードがある。

## E. 政策的含意への質問

### E1. 追加性検証はどう設計し直すべきか
**Q**: "Concretely, how would you fix additionality verification?"
**A**: > Three things follow from the case. **First, define the baseline jointly**: verification should be specified over **the external transfer and the domestic intergovernmental transfer system together**, not over total eligible public expenditure alone — because a member state can hold the latter constant while reshaping the former. **Second, assign a horizontal mandate**: in Finland each ministry executed EU funds as an extension of its own budget lines, and **nobody had either the mandate or the incentive to check across sectors**. Verification needs an actor whose remit is horizontal by design. **Third, publish the counterfactual construction**, whatever it is. The deep problem in this case wasn't concealment — it was that **no institution was charged with constructing the counterfactual at all.**
**骨子**：①**ベースラインを統合的に定義**（外部移転と国内政府間移転制度を一体で。総適格公的支出だけでは、後者を保ったまま前者を作り変えられる）②**水平的権限の付与**（各省が自省予算の延長として執行し、部門横断で確認する権限も誘因も誰も持たなかった）③**反事実の構築方法を公表**（本事例の深い問題は隠蔽ではなく、反事実を構築する任務を負う機関が存在しなかったこと）。

### E2. RRFへの含意
**Q**: "You mention the Recovery and Resilience Facility. Is the parallel really close?"
**A**: > The parallel I claim is **narrow and structural**, not substantive. RRF is performance-based and differently governed, so I'm not saying it repeats Objective 6. What carries over is the **configuration**: a large external transfer landing in national systems that are **themselves being reformed**, with the domestic intergovernmental baseline moving underneath. **My point is about evaluation design, not about the instrument**: an assessment that treats the domestic baseline as given can be satisfied while the aggregate fiscal position of the receiving territories deteriorates — and it will also miss the institutional effect, which in my case is the clearest thing in the data. If anything, the lesson is that **the form in which resources reach subnational actors deserves as much evaluative attention as their volume.**
**骨子**：主張する類似は**狭く構造的**で内容的ではない。RRFは成果ベースでガバナンスも異なるので、目標6の反復とは言わない。移るのは**配置**：大きな外部移転が、**それ自体が改革中の**国内システムに着地し、国内の政府間ベースラインが下で動いている。**論点は手段ではなく評価設計**：国内ベースラインを所与とする評価は、受領地域の集計的財政状況が悪化していても満たされうるし、制度的効果（本事例でデータ上最も明確なもの）も取り逃がす。教訓は、**資源が下位政府に届く「形式」は、その「量」と同じだけ評価上の注意に値する**ということ。

## F. 意地の悪い質問への備え

### F1. 「結局、何も見つかっていないのでは？」
**Q**: "Stripped down, you have a null result and a lot of caveats. What's the finding?"
**A**: > Three findings, and I'd rather state them plainly than dress them up. **One: the functional composition.** Core welfare received 9.2 per cent of EU-source money, and the Objective 6 association is concentrated in economic development — the only association that survives multiple-testing adjustment. That's a positive result about **where the money went**. **Two: the composition of the domestic contraction.** It was concentrated in the least conditional component of the transfer system, on the eve of the programmes. **Three: the null itself, which is a finding.** It **excludes** a specific mechanism — the municipal-ledger swap — that a loose reading of pseudo-complementarity would predict. **That negative result disciplines the substitution claim rather than confirming it**, and it locates the open question where only project-level and ministerial records could answer it. A literature that has mostly worked with aggregates has not previously shown that.
**骨子**：3つある。①**機能構成**（福祉9.2%、目標6の関連は経済開発に集中し、それが調整を生き延びる唯一の関連）——「マネーがどこへ行ったか」についてのポジティブな結果。②**国内収縮の構成**（プログラム前夜に、移転制度で最も条件の緩い部分に集中）。③**ゼロ結果そのものが知見**——疑似補完性の緩い読みが予測する特定メカニズム（自治体台帳スワップ）を**排除する**。この負の結果は代替主張を**確認するのではなく規律づけ**、未解決の問いを、プロジェクト段階と省庁記録だけが答えられる場所に定位する。集計データ中心の文献はこれを示してこなかった。

### F2. 「[AUTHOR TO CONFIRM] が残っているが」
**Q**: (査読者・座長から) "There seem to be unresolved specification details."
**A**: > Yes — the version circulated is the full paper as submitted, and a number of specification details are being finalised for the final version: **the exact PPML specification, the FDR procedure and its test family, the confidence intervals for the grant components, and the boundary vintage used for the Objective 6 crosswalk.** None of them changes the substantive readings I've presented, because the readings rest on **sign and composition rather than magnitude** — but I'd rather flag them than let anyone find them. If any of you have a view on how they should be settled, that's exactly the kind of comment I came for.
**骨子**：はい。回覧版は投稿時の全文で、いくつかの仕様詳細を最終版に向け確定中（**PPMLの正確な仕様、FDR手続と検定族、補助金構成要素の信頼区間、目標6クロスウォークの境界ヴィンテージ**）。提示した実質的読解は**大きさではなく符号と構成**に依拠しているのでいずれも変わらないが、隠すより先に述べる。どう確定すべきか意見があればまさにそれを聞きに来た。

### F3. 「p = 0.048 は都合が良すぎるのでは」
**Q**: "Your one surviving result has an adjusted p of 0.048. That's uncomfortably close to the line."
**A**: > It is, and I don't want to lean on the threshold. **The result I actually rely on isn't the p-value; it's the ordering and the pattern.** Across seven function groups, economic development has the largest Objective 6 association and welfare has the smallest — and Figure 3 shows the development surplus **growing over the period** while the welfare row stays near zero. Those are two independent ways of seeing the same thing, and neither depends on a threshold. If you deleted the p-values from the table, **the functional division of labour would still be the reading**, which is why I state it as a claim about direction and composition rather than significance.
**骨子**：その通りで、閾値に依拠したくない。**実際に依拠しているのはp値ではなく順序とパターン**。7つの機能群で経済開発が最大、福祉が最小の関連を示し、図3は開発余剰が**期間中に拡大**し福祉行はゼロ近傍のままであることを示す。これは同じことを見る2つの独立した方法で、どちらも閾値に依存しない。表からp値を消しても**機能的分業という読解は残る**——だからこれを有意性ではなく方向と構成についての主張として述べている。

### F4. 「政治的解釈が推測的では」
**Q**: "Your account of how the government presented the funds domestically versus to the Commission is reconstruction, not evidence."
**A**: > **Correct, and the paper says so in a footnote and in the limitations.** The internal records of ministries and negotiators have not been examined; statements about how actors understood complementarity and additionality are **reconstructions from published sources** — audit reports, Pirkola's account, contemporary press coverage — **and they are marked as such in the text.** What I do claim without inference is narrower and, I think, safe: **the funds were politically useful to a government pursuing consolidation while protecting core constituencies**, and ratification depended on an agricultural package interlocked with the Objective 6 financial framework. Replacing reconstruction with evidence from negotiation minutes is one of the three things I list as needed next.
**骨子**：**その通りで、脚注と限界に明記している**。省庁・交渉担当者の内部記録は未参照で、行為者の理解についての言明は**公刊資料からの再構成**（監査報告、Pirkola、当時の報道）であり**本文でそう明示**している。推論なしに主張しているのはもっと狭く安全な内容：**基金は、中核支持層を保護しつつ緊縮を進める政府にとって政治的に有用だった**、批准は目標6の財政枠組みと噛み合った農業パッケージに依存していた。交渉議事録による置換は、必要な次の3つの一つ。
