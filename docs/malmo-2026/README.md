# Malmö 2026 — Kitakyushu briefing

Briefing note prepared for **Per-Arne Nilsson** (Senior Strategist, Environmental Department,
City of Malmö), for the meeting in Malmö on **9 September 2026** during the Nordic field study
(7–11 September 2026: Danish Energy Agency · Copenhagen Business School · Malmö · Lolland).

## Rebuilding the PDF

```sh
chromium --headless --disable-gpu --no-sandbox --no-pdf-header-footer \
  --print-to-pdf=kitakyushu-briefing.pdf kitakyushu-briefing.html
```

Any Chromium build works. Japanese glyphs in the source list need a CJK font installed
(IPAGothic is used here).

## Structure of the note

1. Why this case — the Malmö/Kitakyushu parallel, framed around finance and ownership
2. Kitakyushu at a glance — including the 64% industrial share of emissions
3. Four phases of the transition, 1901 to the present
4. The current decarbonisation programme (Green Growth Strategy, Decarbonisation Leading Area,
   the "100% Renewable Kitakyushu Model", progress figures)
5. Third-party ownership / PPA — the mechanism most transferable to a European reader
6. A critical reading, drawn from the author's own research on FAIS and the city's industrial
   structure (flagged in the document as work in progress)
7. Eight questions for the City of Malmö
8. Sources
